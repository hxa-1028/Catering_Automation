# Sample proposal templates

`sample-harbor-template.docx` is a **sanitized demonstration template** for
the prototype only. It contains no client branding, commercial terms, menu
commitments, or real pricing.

The renderer selects it only when the approved venue catalog record has
`template_id: sample-harbor-template`. Its placeholders are limited to the
validated proposal context:

- `proposal.client_name`, `proposal.client_contact`, and `proposal.event_date`;
- `proposal.venue_name`, `proposal.guest_count`, `proposal.event_type`, and
  `proposal.service_styles`;
- each `proposal.line_items` entry (`line_item_id`, `quantity`, `unit`,
  `unit_price`, and `total`); and
- `proposal.total` and `proposal.currency`.

For a client deployment, the regional content owner supplies the approved Word
template for each venue/language/version. Do not use this sample as a client
proposal template or add commercial wording without that approval.
